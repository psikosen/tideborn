# Tideborn Survival Presentation Map

## The judge-facing promise

Within the first minute, the player should understand: **I am a small octopus; winter is coming; I must hunt, gather, excavate, and prepare shelter in a world that reacts to me.**

## Portrait survival hierarchy

1. **World first:** roughly the upper two-thirds of the screen stays gameplay. The octopus, immediate terrain, prey, shelter opening, and danger silhouettes must remain readable without opening panels.
2. **Survival glance:** health, hunger, stamina, and moisture compress into a small top strip. Pressure/depth replaces moisture prominence during deep descent.
3. **Season pressure:** a compact season/storm ribbon shows the current phase, time remaining, and one short objective. It should communicate escalation without a quest checklist covering the world.
4. **Hands-on controls:** the lower third belongs to the left movement pad, right aim/dig region, five essential action buttons, and tappable toolbelt. Buttons use verbs or unmistakable symbols with pressed/cooldown states.
5. **Context over clutter:** interact/grab prompts appear near the octopus or relevant touch button, then disappear. Crafting, atlas, den details, and results use deliberate overlays.

## Session arc

### Early autumn - Learn and forage

- Strong coastal color and generous visibility.
- Immediate loop: swim -> hunt/gather -> eat or save -> find a cavity.
- Feedback: resource pop, short ecosystem message, inventory/toolbelt update.

### Late autumn - Build and choose

- Weather darkens, wind reaches tree crowns, waves roughen, animals reposition.
- The player chooses between exploring toward a seven-den network or investing in one exceptional den.
- Visible den readiness should read as a shelter silhouette plus three simple qualities: space, stores, stability.

### Storm front - Risk and consequence

- Red/amber predator alerts, stronger motion, rain, sediment movement, flooding risk, and louder screen-space effects.
- Opportunity remains visible: washed-up food, fibers, wood, shells, and minerals.
- UI becomes quieter except for critical danger, remaining time, and shelter readiness.

### Winter threshold - Result

- Freeze active play and show one clear outcome: network ready, solo refuge ready, octopus lost, or all shelter lost.
- Summarize dens, best den size/readiness, food stored, tools crafted, creatures hunted, deepest dive, and ecological impact.
- A large thumb-safe **Play Again** button restarts every system and save value.

## Contest polish priorities

1. Touch clarity and camera framing.
2. Understandable survival escalation and reachable ending.
3. Stable frame rate and fast restart.
4. Readable ecological reactions.
5. Deep-sea spectacle as an optional reward, never a requirement for understanding the core loop.
